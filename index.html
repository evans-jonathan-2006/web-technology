How to Make Digital Magazine

If you don’t know how to create an online magazine, it may feel like a daunting task. Don’t worry! FlippingBook online magazine maker is intuitive and easy-to-use, so you can create and publish your magazines online in a few simple steps. No special skills needed.

  1. Upload your PDF magazine to the FlippingBook online magazine creator and customize its design. Set your background, add branding, and choose a color palette.

  2. Add interactivity: embed videos to bring your articles and stories to life, or include links to relevant sources across the pages of your digital magazine.

  3. Place a lead form on any page to grow your audience and encourage people to sign up for more. Pass the data right into your CRM with a Zapier integration.

  4. Publish your emagazine and share it with your readers. You can embed it into your website, distribute it via email and social media, or generate a QR code for it.

  5. Track performance: FlippingBook’s analytics will show you how many people have read your latest issue and even what pages they found especially interesting.
